Who scores, not what to bet

This board ranks players by how likely they are to score. Its top pick is right 51.1% of the time against 16.2% for a candidate drawn at random — about 3.2x the field. Restricted to picks it rates above 0.50 it reaches 61.6% [57.6%, 65.4%], on 47% of games.

Against the bookmaker, same 849 games. The honest yardstick — a price, not a coin flip.
