Re-measured on 1,698 team-games of backtest residuals, teammates' outcomes correlate +0.020 — sharing a scoring game and competing for the same touchdowns very nearly cancel — so the correction would be smaller than the error in measuring it. 6 of these rows are same-game slips, named by their game above. Their price is arithmetic, not a quote — a book prices its own correlation into a same-game parlay and will come back shorter. The strip above is this exact setting replayed over 2023-2025: 1,593 slips, of which 14.1% landed against the 14.9% we said beforehand.
